# Open Peeps onboarding figures

Seven complete sitting templates in `scenes/` (one per setup screen) by Pablo
Stanley (https://openpeeps.com), released into the public domain under CC0 1.0
Universal: copy, remix and commercial use allowed, no attribution required and
none needed.

Each template is used exactly as drawn — its own integrated head, never
covered, composited, or replaced. In particular the person's Notion avatar is
never drawn onto a figure. From `roadmap/references/illustrations/Flat Assets`
(Templates/Sitting, mono set: black strokes, white fills, transparent ground).

Rendered in-process (same `NSImage(contentsOf:)` raster path as the Notion
avatar parts); nothing is fetched, converted, or uploaded at runtime.
