## The promise and the current boundary

The site says **“Be present. Keep your promises.”** It shows one Next Notes moving
between a family calendar question, dictation in a work app, a Mac app action and a
meeting follow-up. The animated scenes are illustrative; they are not evidence that
the whole chain works. Today the Mac dictation and meeting paths have real use, while
the Agent's Phase 1 live gate is still below 25/30, Phases 2–4 are open, and durable
Agent jobs are not built. A calendar-armed recording is real proactivity; a remembered
obligation checked later and resolved without a prompt is still planned. The iMessage
command channel and phone calls are separate unfinished work per `README.md` (recheck
their state before implementing because that work may be active elsewhere).

**Position:** one familiar companion, beginning on the Mac. “Home helper,” “office
associate” and “meeting partner” describe the job in a moment, not three identities,
personas, conversations, memories or permissions. The same name, voice and visual
character continue across supported surfaces; the relevant history is available and
editable where the person expects it. A capability may be local or cloud by explicit
workload choice; its source and consent must remain visible. Do not claim a phone,
24/7 cloud worker, universal app control or autonomous life management until a real
path proves it.

## Additive work (X IDs are promise exits, not replacement P tasks)

### X01 — One identity across surfaces

Inventory Agent pane, voice, island, meeting panel, notifications, dictation HUD and
future message/phone channels. Use the existing `AgentIdentity`/avatar source and the
existing session/task identifiers. Remove role-specific names that make “home helper”
and “office associate” look like separate agents. A role label can change with the
task; identity, consent and the task do not. Keep dictation's output profile distinct
from the companion's personality. **Gate:** a fixture walks home → office → meeting →
voice and asserts one display name, one active identity and no duplicate task or
memory row; an offscreen sheet checks light/dark, narrow/wide and reduced motion.
Depends on P4-03 and P3-09 for the final voice/island ownership switch; the visual
inventory may start now. Coordinate with `roadmap/in-progress/WEBSITE-TO-MAC-EXPERIENCE/`.

### X02 — History a person can actually use

The same person must be able to find the question, the work, the decision and the
finished result after changing context or relaunching. Fix the Agent pane's 40-row
display cap that hides older retained messages and the scroll jump when new events
arrive (`REVIEW-LOG.md` §10). Link a meeting, suggestion, task and approval back to
their source using existing ids; show why a fact is remembered and let the person
correct or remove it. A history is not a second store. **Gate:** fixture with >40
messages, two meetings and one task survives relaunch; search/jump opens the right
source; new events do not pull a reader away; delete/correct is reflected in later
answers. Depends on P1-18/26/30, P4-07/11 and P6-02/08.

### X03 — Honest, calm work-in-progress and control

The app shows whether it is listening, reading, drafting, waiting for review, acting,
done, blocked or uncertain, with the specific next action in ordinary words. A
typing animation or avatar pose reflects a real event and never simulates progress.
Read operations proceed; one approval is reserved for irreversible writes. Keep the
proposal, account/target, source and outcome visible; allow edit, decline and stop;
surface an unknown write outcome without retrying it. Fix unreachable finished-task
result links noted in `REVIEW-LOG.md` §10. **Gate:** crash/reopen, approved, declined,
cancelled and unknown-outcome fixtures each show the correct final state and artifact;
voice interruption does not kill ongoing work. Depends on P1-19–23, P3-03/08/11,
P6-04/06/08 and website-to-Mac UI work. No new Activity ledger.

### X04 — Three real jobs, end to end

Use these as the product's customer journeys, not a new benchmark replacing the 30
canonical tool cases: (A) family calendar answer grounded in live calendar data;
(B) dictated office reply lands in the captured app; (C) meeting decision becomes a
reviewed Mac app update. Pin fixture versions with wrong day, wrong person, duplicate
meeting title, missing permission, changed account, interrupted run and uncertain
write. Then run attended owner tests with private details kept out of reports. **Gate:**
every leg reaches a verified result or a specific honest stop on two consecutive
runs; no false “sent,” “added” or “clear afternoon”; capture observed time-to-first-
feedback and completion. The Phase 1 >=25/30 and owner examples remain independent
requirements. Depends on P1 gate, P3-11, P6-06 and X03; dictation leg can pass early.

### X05 — A promise from meeting to resolution

Meeting arming and notes already exist. Complete the specific loop shown on the site:
recognize the promised action with quotation/source, present an editable follow-up,
send only after approval, verify the result, and recognize later completion before
nagging. Show what remains owed and what was done. A post-meeting wrap cannot claim
an action happened because a draft exists. **Gate:** one real meeting and fixtures
for repeat mention, amended promise, skipped recording, notes failure, draft edit,
send failure and externally completed action; one owed item, one proposal, one
confirmed outcome, no stale nudge. Depends on P4-04–06/10/13, P6-06/08 and X02–X03.

### X06 — Proactive help worth hearing from

Pre-meeting brief, morning offer, owed-item nudge and one useful next-step offer are
already specified in Phase 4. Measure whether each is timely, grounded, concise and
quiet when nothing is actionable. The user's calendar, mail and memory consents are
source-specific. Respect quiet hours and a dismissal; never turn every meeting into
a notification. **Gate:** in a seven-day fixture schedule, all expected prompts appear
once, stale/completed/duplicate prompts appear zero times, quiet hours are respected,
and an absent permission gives a plain attended path. Owner trial records accepted,
dismissed and wrong suggestions without content in `usage.jsonl`. Depends on Phase 4
and X05. Do not add a scheduler or usage log.

### X07 — Speed and full-duplex as part of the product

Preserve Phase 2's <=0.9 s warm speech-end-to-first-audio median, <=200 ms duck and
<=50 ms speech gap gates, and Phase 3's rule that a follow-up revises work rather than
silently cancelling it. Extend the attended journeys to measure *perceived* first
feedback: key-down, recording armed, task accepted, source found and approval ready.
No fixed wait, extra approval or serial model pass may be added to make the UI look
smooth. **Gate:** phase latency/full-duplex flags and the X04/X05 journey reports show
first feedback at the immediate event, no frozen >10 s work state and no lost typed
or spoken correction. Depends on Phases 2–3 and X03.

### X08 — Reach beyond the open Mac, only with truthful availability

One companion should be reachable when a person leaves the desk. Finish and verify
the existing iMessage command-channel work before promising it; preserve pairing,
sender authority, device-offline behavior, approval continuity and attachment limits.
Phone calls are a separate explicit path, with identity and consent resolved before
outbound calls; do not imply they ship because a design exists. Evaluate whether a
mobile client is needed after testing message and voice reach; neither a phone app nor
a second agent is a prerequisite for Mac-first launch. **Gate:** a paired-device live
message request returns to the same task/history and cannot execute another sender's
approval; offline Mac is reported honestly; a phone-call claim requires an attended
placed/declined/missed-call suite. Coordinate with active iMessage/call work and X01–X03.

### X09 — First useful moment without setup homework

Show one plain, guided path from install to a successful hold or meeting, then an
optional calendar/Workspace connection as needed. Grants are requested at the moment
their benefit is clear; the default path needs no account, key or Terminal. The first
review card explains exactly what will happen and remains until decided. **Gate:**
fresh-profile tests for grant accepted, denied, later granted, no model available,
offline and no meeting yet; each reaches a useful action or one clear next click, with
no raw tool names. Depends on P1-16, P4-13, existing `--selftest-onboarding`,
`--selftest-guided` and X03.

### X10 — Privacy, provenance and safety proof

Make local/optional-cloud boundaries legible per Agent, meetings and dictation.
Prove every displayed claim with the source actually read; side talk must not become
the owner's memory; personal and professional contexts may share one *identity*
without silently sharing a cloud consent. Test account changes, revoked permissions,
wrong recipient, stale approval, unknown external write and data removal. **Gate:**
existing store-isolation/permission/memory tests plus end-to-end negative cases for
X04/X05; the UI names the source and the exact outgoing content without leaking it
into logs. Depends on P1-19/21/30, P4-07/10/13, P6-06 and SHARED-BRAIN consent work.

### X11 — Real-world proof and a claim gate for the website

Maintain a small claim ledger with site sentence, production path, proof, last date
and limitation. Re-run X04/X05 when copy changes; label animated walkthroughs as
examples. The site must not say “sent,” “added,” “always working,” “calls,” or “one
history” for a path that only drafted, simulated, requires the Mac awake, or hides
older content. Add usability sessions with people outside the repo: can they finish
one home and one office job without instruction, find an old result, explain an
approval, and know what was done? Track first success, task completion, incorrect
claims and seven-day repeat use with aggregated, content-free counts; use the one
`usage.jsonl` for local diagnostics and ask for consent before any external analytics.
**Gate:** every hero scene has dated end-to-end evidence or narrower wording; zero
known false completion claims; three outside users complete the guided journey and
can identify the app's limits. Depends on X01–X10; copy can be corrected earlier.

The exit for this document is not eleven checkboxes alone: a new person can ask for
help in one context, return in another, recognize the same companion, inspect what
it did, correct it, and see a real outcome without learning an engineering term.
