## Decisions and deviations

- 2026-09-26 M-14: the red-first cadence case counted **7** passes where the task text said "today ≥ 8". The fixture is the task's (2 min, 2 s finals, 60 segments) and the old rule is a burst of 8, so 60/8 = 7 complete bursts and the seventh leaves 4 finals behind — the assertion the task asked for (≤ 3) still fails, by more than double, and the honest number is recorded rather than the fixture widened to make the sentence true. A second case was added that the task did not name, because the 45 s gap has to be pinned from the other side: 2 min of *continuous* speech scheduled 15 passes and now schedules 2.
- 2026-09-26 M-14: the store's evidence buffer is 360 segments, sized from `windowMinutes` (`= windowMinutes * 60 * 2`) rather than kept at 80. The task's file list did not mention it, but 80 segments is ~2.7 minutes at two-second finals and ~80 s at one-second finals: a buffer shorter than the window turns the three-minute window back into a length proxy through the back door, and the 3,000-character cap would then be cutting a window that was never three minutes. It holds transcript text, not audio, and is cleared with the meeting.
- 2026-09-26 M-14: `isEnabled(stored:choice:)` takes the effective `ModelRoleChoice` rather than an `LLMProviderID`. Target 3 names `.appLLM`/`.appleFoundation`, and the mapping is one to one (`.builtIn` and `.installedModel` → `.appLLM`, `.appleFoundation` → itself, `.cloud` → `.openRouter`, `.localServer` → itself); taking the role's decision is what lets the gate reuse P0-14's read-only resolution instead of introducing a second one, and `provider(for: .agent)` then hands back the provider that judgement was about. `.app` is off, and is unreachable for this role anyway — `ModelRoleStore.resolve` sends an agent app to the built-in model first.
- 2026-09-26 M-14: one rule checked in two places, deliberately. The store asks before it arms a debounced pass (so automatic mode with an online model schedules nothing at all), and `ModelCompleter.refine` asks again before it resolves a provider (so nothing that reaches the completer from anywhere else can spend a model call on a cloud). This is the permission model's own shape — the manifest answers both the planner and the executor — and it is one pure function, not two answers. `--selftest-meeting-live-tools` injects a completer, so the store's gate is the one that has to hold for a fake.
