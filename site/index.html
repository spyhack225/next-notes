One familiar companion, starting on your Mac

Be present. Keep your promises.

Next Notes helps with a family plan, a reply at work, and the promise you made in a meeting. It is the same companion through each moment. Home helper, office associate, and meeting partner are examples of how it helps, not separate assistants.

Download for Mac · Free and open source · Requires macOS 26 on Apple silicon

Help where you already work

  • Dictate in your Mac apps. Hold a key, speak naturally, and your words appear where your cursor was.
  • Ask about your day. The Mac app can read your calendar when you ask, so you can make a family or work plan.
  • Keep meeting notes. With calendar recording on, an eligible meeting can be recorded and summarized without a bot joining the call.
  • Review the follow-up. With connected work apps enabled, Next Notes can propose a next step for your approval.

Your choices stay visible

Speech and notes can be handled locally on your Mac. Optional cloud models and connected services send the information needed for the feature you choose. Agent-proposed actions that send or change something are shown for review before they happen.
