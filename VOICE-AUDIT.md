## Fresh install, saved preference, and missing voices today

Source-defined keys are `agentVoiceEngine`, `agentVoiceIdentifier`, `agentPocketVoice` (`Support/Settings.swift:1262–1263,1283`); Settings uses UserDefaults.standard in production (:1157). App bundle domain is `ai.pivotstudio.nextnotes` (`Support/AppIdentity.swift:4`), with older-domain migration in that file. **I did not read defaults or this owner's saved values. This Mac's actual saved selection is therefore unknown by design.** There is no separate “this Mac default” in source: saved `apple`/missing/unknown → Apple; saved `pocket` → Apple until Pocket ready, then Pocket on the next reply; saved supported `kokoro` → Kokoro prepare/synthesize, Apple if it fails; unsupported Kokoro → Apple immediately. Empty Apple identifier leaves system voice choice; nonempty identifier is passed to AVSpeechSynthesisVoice.

On a fresh Mac with neither model present, the default live reply speaks Apple immediately. Pocket is not proactively downloaded merely because the fresh default is Apple. Selecting Pocket in Settings prepares/downloads it (UI says about 550 MB), and selection changes only on success; the previous Apple choice remains usable during failure/preparation. A saved Pocket choice starts asynchronous prepare when the shared synthesizer is first constructed, but every reply begun before readiness speaks Apple for that whole reply. It does not switch voices halfway just because readiness arrives. Supported selected Kokoro may leave a person waiting during download/load/synthesis; failure uses Apple. There is no general visible “using Apple because your voice isn't ready” message from the selector; failures are logged.

Catch-up is different: text is delivered immediately by the transform (:323); rendering runs separately. With default Apple and no allowed engine already ready, no audio artifact is made. The error appears only in a log (:347). A person receives text and may wonder why there is no recording. Selected Pocket may wait through its cold load (comment estimates 10–30s); allowed-voice load failure leaves text without audio. These are source-based outcomes, not runtime timing measurements made for this audit.

## Exact minimal removal proposal (no implementation in this audit)

1. **Close both central routes.** `AgentSpeechSynthesizer.swift:101,205–215,313–327,521–529,630–655`: remove Apple backing ownership and initialization; initial/restore state must be non-speaking/unavailable. Select only ready Pocket or ready supported Kokoro. Unready/unknown/unsupported returns explicit unavailable/preparing state; never selects systemBacking. Replace error fallback with cancellation of remaining speech, truthful interrupted/unheard settlement and a visible availability message. Clear clause/token/reply/pause/first-audio state and settle the same output owner. Do not emit startAcknowledged/completed or mark delivery spoken for a visual reply. Keep fake fallback injection only if testing an allowed fallback policy.
2. **Remove the sink and bypasses.** Delete AVSpeechBacking and Apple-only wiring at `AgentSpeechSynthesizer.swift:1024–1462` after replacing its diagnostic callers/types with generic PCM receipts as needed. Remove Apple choices/construction in `AcousticLiveProbe.swift:20–25` and `AcousticHybridProbe.swift:321–334`. An `apple` diagnostic argument must explicitly fail input validation, never secretly choose another voice. This makes Apple TTS unreachable even through command-line tests. The separate `Tools/tts_benchmark.swift:328–329,378–379` Apple benchmark should also be removed/disabled if the owner means repository-wide use; it is outside the shipping app.
3. **Migrate selection and remove its UI.** `Support/Settings.swift:1436,857–858`: default to Pocket, normalize legacy `apple`, missing and invalid values to Pocket, and validate future assignments to Pocket/Kokoro. Preserve a valid supported Kokoro choice; expose its OS unavailability rather than silently selecting Apple. Stop using `agentVoiceIdentifier` for synthesis. `ModelsSettingsTab.swift:786–1010`: remove Apple tile, picker, enumeration/quality code and “macOS voices work now” copy. Store the allowed requested choice immediately when selected so failed preparation cannot retain Apple; show preparation/download/error state and retry. A requested choice is not readiness.
4. **Keep the screen useful.** At `RealtimeAudioSession.swift:200–258` and `OutputScheduler.swift:169–188,593–604`, consume the central availability/failure state for ALL outputs, including sessionless preview/read-aloud. Keep ordinary answer text in the conversation/Island and add: **“Your voice is getting ready. You can read the answer here.”** Missing files: **“Download a voice to hear replies.”** Error: **“Voice couldn't start. Your answer is here.”** Include a Settings/retry action. Put progress in the existing speaking-back UI (`ModelsSettingsTab.swift:853–880`) and existing reply presentation, not only a log. With no ready allowed voice, choose text immediately instead of an unbounded voice wait. When ready, speak only the next reply; do not unexpectedly play a stale answer. Preserve unheard task results/questions for explicit replay and approval prompts for real playback/review; no automatic spoken receipt on text-only settlement.
5. **Prepare at launch, not first reply.** `NextNotesApp.swift:486` (applicationDidFinishLaunching, after its diagnostic/startup isolation guards): invoke the existing owned Pocket preparation once in normal launch, asynchronously, after allowed choice normalization. `AgentSpeechSynthesizer.swift:257–265` already starts it at lazy synthesizer construction; share that owner's prepareTask (`PocketAgentVoice.swift:113–150`), not a second load. Keep shutdown cancellation/join and resource policy. Show downloading/preparing status until isReady (after warm prediction). If default auto-download is not approved, launch should prepare only cached assets and show the download action. This reduces cold first-reply delay once warm, but cannot make missing files ready immediately. Pocket's comment :153–157 reports roughly .55s first prediction vs .08s after warmup on this machine; treat these as historical source estimates, not a new guarantee. Launch preparation consumes memory/compute and must coexist with dictation/meeting admission.
6. **Make Catch-up absence visible.** `CatchUpTemplate.swift:345–347`: preserve delivered text and attach a plain audio-unavailable/preparing state to the existing task/artifact presentation. `LongFormRenderer.swift:429–443` already never uses Apple; update comments/normalized-choice handling, retain file-only rendering. Do not turn render failure into a live spoken Apple explanation.

## Risks and owner decisions

- **Fresh Mac:** choose whether first launch automatically downloads the roughly 550 MB Pocket voice, or asks for Download while answering in text. Either is compatible with the no-Apple rule; an unexplained silent voice button is not.
- **VoiceOver:** removing app Apple TTS should not disable accessibility labels, notifications, or the user's screen reader. Decide whether the rule covers only app-generated voice (recommended interpretation) or also externally configured VoiceOver; the app cannot promise control over another application's/system voice.
- **Hands-free use:** text-only replies need an accessible visible state and explicit replay after setup. A person away from the screen loses spoken reminders when no allowed voice is ready; retain notifications and text rather than falsely claiming read-aloud delivery.
- **Permissions and approvals:** an unheard prompt must not arm a spoken “yes” approval as if the person heard it. Preserve the existing pending proposal and its visual review flow.
- **Kokoro OS guard:** maintain the existing exclusion for macOS 26.4–26.5. Download availability does not imply runtime safety. Pocket/text remains the usable route.
- **Cold-start/pressure:** proactive Pocket preparation can improve first reply but competes with capture/models on this 16 GB Mac. Keep one owned load, cancellation/join, and visible waiting/error states. No new latency claim until native allowed-voice acceptance runs.
- **Fallback preference:** simplest minimal policy is text when the selected allowed voice fails. Switching automatically to an already-ready other allowed engine is also legal but changes the person's chosen voice; owner should decide before adding that complexity.
- **Tests:** deleting Apple native fixtures loses Apple-specific callback coverage that no longer matters to shipping speech. Replace shared ownership invariants and retain real allowed-engine/WAV playback acceptance; never present fake playback as native proof.

Audit complete as a source/history review; removal is proposed, not implemented or runtime-verified.
