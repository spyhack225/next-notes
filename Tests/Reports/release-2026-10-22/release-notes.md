Next Notes 0.2.24 brings the reviewed main changes into the app and adds a shared benchmark hub.

- Compare speech models by measured speed, size and accuracy, with language-based automatic choices. Existing saved choices are retained.
- Browse reviewed speech, voice and workflow results on the benchmark page, with measurement conditions and remaining limits.
- Includes reviewed streaming-output and Google connection repairs, plus scoped task-history, meeting-coverage and playback checks.

Benchmark timings have different boundaries: generated audio, paced transcription and physical speaker output are separate measurements. This release does not declare a fastest audible voice or resolve the open aggregate Agent and meeting-quality gates.

Requires Apple silicon and macOS 26. This development build is locally signed; it is not notarized by Apple.

Validation and exact source/binary receipts: [release record](https://github.com/spyhack225/next-notes/tree/main/Tests/Reports/release-2026-10-22).
