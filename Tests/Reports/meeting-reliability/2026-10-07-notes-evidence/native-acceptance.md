## Explicit source-ID experiment

This flag is distinct from --notes-selection-indices, which selects the failed whole extractive diagnostic. Preserve actual per-map/collapse raw arrays, admitted count, finish-by-limit and incomplete markers in private output. Review both the source selections and final notes against the original full transcript and private reference notes. A correct selection can still be misrepresented by the final reduce. Use a private row per gold fact with source ID/time/speaker, admitted map/group, final-note representation, classification, unsupported additions and verdict. Do not copy these utterances or notes into this public document.

| Gate | Required original-flow evidence | Reject condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Experience attribution | Both speakers' distinct experience and qualifications attached to the original speaker | Prototype and production/sourcing experience transferred between speakers, or speaker identity guessed |
| Coverage | Main substantive distinctions and explanations throughout the full call preserved | Greetings/opening-only selection, generic topic labels, missing late material, or a larger count treated as proof |

| Decisions/actions/questions | Classification follows the actual utterance and its question/answer context | Greeting, explanation or hypothetical example classified as an action; resolved question presented as open |
| Readability | Usable summary and enough concrete detail to review the meeting | Bare greetings, transcript fragments with missing antecedents, or unreadable excerpt dump |
| Structural honesty | Actual cut/refusal/drop notice retained where applicable | incompleteFacts=false or generation OK cited as a semantic coverage verdict |
| Cost | Same fixture/provider/window, measured input/output budget, wall/workflow time, passes and collapse/drop | Quality loss accepted solely for speed; extra context/passes hidden; instant/perfect claim without proof |

There is no minimum-ID quota and no automatic checker/retry/model pass. More selected utterances may raise reduce/context cost. Record it honestly; retain failures. Full-input whole diagnostics must pass the actual preflight context count and remain experimental. The current Mini and Qwen whole-index failures are negative controls, not candidates for default promotion.
