## Separate acceptance layers and future installed tests

**Producer → shadow** belongs to the assigned Agent Overhaul closure work. Its currently proposed scope is bounded: confirmed pause (including queued output), late firstAudio while paused retaining heard/paused, matched interruption/finish preserving proposal priority, and responseEnded preserving already-visible acknowledged speaking. No installed original/fixed result has been supplied for that proposal yet. Resume receipt/epoch, late pre-pause receipts delivered after resume and approvalResolved restoring speaking remain open; inputDiscarded/resume status cannot change independently of the backend receipt and legacy projection. A generic precedence helper cannot infer active playback from historical heard/playing. Any later success establishes only its exact tested producer/shadow contracts, **not** `IslandState.kind`, `IslandView`, buttons or pending approval. P3-06a must pass its own shadow acceptance; the current STATUS still calls it in progress. P3-03a/b must own tasks before P3-09's worker write gate, per the roadmap dependency (`04-PHASE-3-FULL-DUPLEX.md:4163–4169`; STATUS P3 rows).

**Producer → live island** starts only with the coordinated P3-09 switch. Its installed regression must follow the real producer output identity through `VoiceSession.publishIsland` to actual `IslandState.kind`/`cardIdentity`, `avatarState`, `ActivationController.mode`, and the `IslandView` controls. Run an original red on the unchanged acceptance fixture before the consumer fix; do not replace the failure with a hand-built `.apply(.agentSpeaking)` or fake event. Minimum scenarios:

1. Open real voice session with no audio yet: immediate listening feedback. Enqueue a clause: still no speaking portrait. Matched positive playback receipt: speaking Kind/portrait/label, one mark, no borrowed listening meter or old `lastReply` text; a microphone indicator, if shown, follows actual capture. Wrong session/output/generation or stale callback: no change.
2. Confirmed provisional pause during playback: speaking clears to user-floor listening without cancelling background work or closing capture; resume returns to speaking only on producer-confirmed post-resume playback for the same output. Hard barge, natural drain, replacement and session close remove the old speaking card; old A cannot clear new B.
3. While speaking, present one real `PermissionGate` request: the actionable proposal/persistent pending badge and exact Approve/Review/Dismiss routing remain reachable after notice expiry. Check the existing listening-over-pending and dictation-over-pending priority deliberately; do not silently change it or let a result notice bury the gate. Background worker progress never changes the conversation portrait/card during an open session; after close, legacy background cards work.
4. Exercise the actual speaking action once its owner is defined: stopping speech affects exactly the matched output and leaves ongoing unrelated tasks and approval authority intact. “Done” still closes the capture session. A natural completed reply in an open session leaves the session ready for another turn; closing later shows the current readable result/closing notice, with no premature `.done` pose during audio.
5. Repeat on a notched and a floating island, collapsed/expanded, Reduce Motion and VoiceOver. Inspect native first-visible timing, whether the panel takes focus, one-mark motion, labels and buttons. `--selftest-island` and `--selftest-avatar` are necessary regressions but their fixture `IslandState.apply` path alone is not end-to-end evidence. Preserve voice duplex and latency gates without extra awaits, clocks, stores, queues or a second confirmation.
