Why and how we tested

How long do you wait for readable words and for the final transcript after releasing dictation? This test compares the model paths inside the signed app, before cleanup and insertion.

Signed optimized application factory, segmented engine, scheduler and transcript consumer;20ms paced authored PCM

The same authored English or French excerpt is fed into the actual application factory and segmented engine in 20 ms pieces. Three warmed paced holds are retained per supported language.

First words are measured at the transcript consumer and include the initial 400 ms audio window. Release-to-final includes outstanding inference. Cold preparation and the physical microphone, display, cleanup and insertion are excluded.

  • hardware: Apple M3,16GB RAM
  • os: macOS27.2 build26B5091g
  • workload: Normal desktop apps; coordinated serial window with no concurrent native build/model job. Existing swap/desktop pressure retained in per-model context; no concurrent voice qualification.
  • power_thermal: Per-model power/thermal/load/swap observations retained. Cold preparation is separate from warmed timing.

Tested build: 0.2.24, signed build 800

Limitations

One7.25sEnglish/6.65sFrench authored excerpt, three timing trials; not a broad corpus or percentile estimate.

Physical microphone, visible Settings/display, cleanup, insertion, concurrent ASR+voice and M2 hardware are unverified.

First readable provisional text may change; this is not stable-word commitment. Warm timings exclude download, cold graph compilation/load and shape warmup.

Short app final-word scoring is separate from the earlier136/151-word runtime accuracy fixture. Do not relabel earlier accuracy as current full-app quality.

Default selection is the owner-requested PhononEnglish/VozMulti setting; Whistle has the lowest raw French latency in this short app run.
