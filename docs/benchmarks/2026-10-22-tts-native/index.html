Measurements

    Why and how we tested

    How quickly can this voice produce its first usable audio buffer? This isolates generation from the separate wait for playback and sound.

    Five completed native generated-PCM comparisons plus a prepared separate physical speaker-start collector.

    Known English text is sent through the actual native voice adapter after preparation. Five back-to-back warm same-text requests are summarized separately from the first synthesis after preparation.

    The clock stops at the first nonempty generated PCM buffer. It does not stop at a speaker, a microphone, or the end of a conversational turn.

    • hardware: Apple M3, 16 GB RAM
    • workload: One engine/model process at a time

    Limitations

    Generated PCM timings are separate from actual non-silent render, estimated presentation and microphone-observed pickup.

    The speaker collector has authored native controls, including an original/fixed maximum-latency search regression; physical model collection awaits a coordinated optimized app build.

    One disposable TTS model/process at a time; each result is saved and its owned model removed before proceeding.

    Full-turn speech-end/ASR/EOU, physical route matrix, voice quality and default qualification remain open.
