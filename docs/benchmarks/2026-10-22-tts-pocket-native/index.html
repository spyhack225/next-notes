Why and how we tested

How quickly can this voice produce its first usable audio buffer? This isolates generation from the separate wait for playback and sound.

Known English text request to first non-empty generated PCM through the actual native adapter; separate muted native playback checks for Qwen/Phonon.

Known English text is sent through the actual native voice adapter after preparation. Five back-to-back warm same-text requests are summarized separately from the first synthesis after preparation.

The clock stops at the first nonempty generated PCM buffer. It does not stop at a speaker, a microphone, or the end of a conversational turn.

  • hardware: Apple M3,16GB RAM
  • os: Version 27.2 (Build 26B5091g)
  • workload: Standalone TTS; no concurrent native benchmark/build, normal owner apps retained, inherited swap pressure. ASR-plus-voice reference workload unqualified.
  • power_thermal: Resume snapshot retained; continuous thermal/power state during trials unmeasured.
  • cold_warm: One first synthesis after preparation, five back-to-back warm runs of same text. Not hardware-cold.

Limitations

Generated PCM is not audible output or speech-end-to-answer latency.

Same-text back-to-back warm runs; human-paced performance, language quality and full-duplex reference acceptance remain open.

Normal owner apps and inherited swap pressure retained; no default promotion or installed-app acceptance.

Child peak RSS excludes external host services and is not Mach physical footprint.
