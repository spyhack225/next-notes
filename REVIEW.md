## Fresh Mac and failure recovery

Normal launch calls the same preparation owner after diagnostic returns (`Sources/NextNotes/NextNotesApp.swift:587,591`). With absent Pocket assets and normal admitted resources, that owner downloads, initializes and performs an unheard warm prediction (`PocketAgentVoice.swift:220,233,239`). While preparing, answers remain text and show “Your voice is getting ready. You can read the answer here.” The Agent feed includes Voice settings (`UI/Agent/AgentView.swift:538,542`); Island reply text uses the output snapshot (`UI/Island/IslandView.swift:488`). Resource deferral shows the same line without numerical progress. No reply is queued for replay when readiness changes; the next reply can use the selected ready engine (`AgentSpeechSynthesizer.swift:372,420`). Nothing speaks during download or the warmup.

Download/load error publishes the owner's error and failed availability, so replies show “Voice couldn't start. Your answer is here.” Pressure/activity events cannot clear a genuine model failure. File/playback failure latches the synth policy even with a cached ready owner; stop and ordinary new replies do not clear it. The explicit backend API can clear it, subject to finding 2's late-failure race and finding 3's missing UI consumer. The currently committed screen also exposes raw owner errors. Settings integration is therefore essential to a usable first-launch failure/retry flow. A healthy fresh Mac's real download, visible progress and physical speech were not observed.
